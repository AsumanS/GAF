# Design system

## Direction

Art and culture nonprofit website.

## Homepage actions

- **Donate** — primary action (header and hero)
- **Volunteer** — secondary action (header and hero)
- Nonprofit status line appears **once** in the hero: “A 501(c)(3) public charity”

## Donation section

Owner-supplied support text and required legal language only. Do not repeat nonprofit status badges there.

## Typography

- Display: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif
- Body / UI: system sans

## Accessibility

WCAG 2.2 AA targets remain in force.
