## Rules

- No AI-generated portraits of real people.
- No fake IRS letters, board lists, partner logos, or manuscript forgeries.
- No generic stock photos of libraries, volunteers, or “hands on old books” as credibility substitutes.
- Development placeholders must not appear in production builds (`MediaFigure` collapses when `src` is empty and `import.meta.env.PROD` is true).
